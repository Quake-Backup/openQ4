# openQ4 0.10.000

## Highlights

- **A new Arena Campaign joins single-player.** Choose **Arena** after selecting
  **Single Player** to fight through five escalating tiers and twenty curated
  matches, while **Mission** continues to open the original Quake 4 story.
- **Single-player menus now feel like one suite.** The Single Player path
  cleanly hands off from the main menu into Mission or Arena, the Demo Library
  uses the same list-and-preview language as Load Game, and shared rails,
  panels, and controls make moving between those screens feel deliberate on
  both wide and tall displays.
- **Long names and translations stay readable.** Demo rows now preserve their
  columns while the selected item remains clearly identified below the list;
  Arena uses concise localized card labels, larger briefing space, and compact
  action controls so translated text remains clear at the menu's intended size.
- **Every tier has its own climb and boss.** Win the first three matches to
  reveal the boss fight, then defeat the boss to unlock the next tier. Wins are
  saved, completed matches remain replayable, and a separate reset action
  leaves Mission saves untouched.
- **The circuit is easier to read and safer to manage.** Tier briefings, a
  visual progress bar, effective bot-skill previews, stronger boss markers,
  explicit next/retry/replay actions, neutral draw results, and confirmation
  before resetting progress make each choice clear before it takes effect.
- **The full openQ4 bot cast enters the circuit.** Hand-picked opponents,
  distinct play styles, per-character skill variation, and a final duel with
  the Makron make the ladder grow from approachable rookie fights into an
  endgame challenge.
- **Multiplayer bots now coordinate around the match, the map, and each other.**
  In Standard CTF, One Flag, Arena CTF and Arena One Flag they divide returns,
  interceptions, escorts, attacks and base defence instead of chasing one flag
  as a pack. Freeze Tag rescuers spread across frozen teammates, while DeadZone
  teams react to artifacts, ownership and deadlocks. Routes repair around live
  obstructions and use ordinary walk-activated doors; combatants choose spacing
  for their weapon, pursue recent sightings, dodge from either side, lead and
  avoid projectiles with their real trajectories, and protect moving teammates
  from direct or splash fire. Batched bot joins also balance before the players'
  first spawn instead of placing every new opponent on one side.
- **Classic combat modes keep the ladder varied.** Duel, Deathmatch, Team
  Deathmatch, Red Rover, and Clan Arena rotate through free-for-all, team, and
  elimination rules instead of repeating one match format.
- **Every match now feels like an event.** Arena skips multiplayer ready-up and
  opens with a cinematic challenge entrance, then freezes the final battlefield
  for a depth-of-field victory orbit before flowing through a staged match
  report and a clearly animated ladder update.

## Upgrade Notes

- No extra maps or replacement assets are required. The Arena Campaign uses
  the official Quake 4 maps already required by openQ4 and does not depend on
  the optional community map pack.
- Existing Mission savegames and ordinary multiplayer settings are not reset.
  Arena progress is new in this release and begins at the first tier.
- If an Arena card reports a missing required map, verify the retail Quake 4
  files and content paths before installing loose map replacements.

## Change Log

- Made bindings easier to recognize in menus and during play. Settings rows now
  summarize keyboard, mouse, and controller bindings with labeled keycaps and
  neutral device graphics, while supported prompts follow the input device used
  most recently; the multiplayer spectator HUD uses the same presentation for
  its follow controls. Escape, Start, and Back now cancel bind capture without
  erasing the action, while Backspace and Delete clear it deliberately.
- Added runtime map entity-string companions for mods and servers. A `.ent`
  now replaces the complete entity string, while `.entx` appends validated
  point entities without modifying or redistributing the original map.
- Added a localized Mission-or-Arena choice beneath the Single Player menu.
- Unified the Demo Library, Single Player selector, and Arena browser around
  Quake 4's stock menu rails, grid, framed panels, and action controls. Tiled
  full-bleed chrome now fills extra screen space without stretching the
  readable menu cards, with input-safe transitions between views and back to
  the main menu.
- Reframed Arena's reset, progress, and result dialogs around the familiar
  legacy confirmation silhouette while retaining their richer staged reveals
  and clearer campaign feedback.
- Polished final menu motion and hierarchy: active Demo filters remain visibly
  selected, the playback deck rises in from the lower rail, and Arena launch,
  result, and progression panels now close behind a composed black layer
  instead of exposing individual nested elements during their exit.
- Added five named tiers with four authored matches apiece, localized match
  briefings, map and rule summaries, roster previews, lock states, and
  completion feedback.
- Added deterministic campaign rosters using all sixteen character bots, with
  tier skill rising from 1 through 5 and small per-opponent offsets.
- Expanded general multiplayer bot intelligence with independent movement and
  combat intent, inventory- and path-aware pickup scoring with soft team
  reservations, goal hysteresis, progress-based recovery, full stock-and-
  expansion weapon choices, and team assignment that reserves pending slots in
  the correct gameplay instance.
- Made navigation react to the live map: selected graph edges are collision-
  checked and repaired around movers, ordinary unlocked walk-activated doors
  remain traversable while gated doors remain barriers, jumps and drops carry
  deliberate travel penalties, and a scaled lower-bound A* heuristic preserves
  cost-correct choices across cheap jump-pad and teleporter links. Roaming also
  retries alternate reachable destinations when one candidate becomes blocked.
- Made projectile combat account for the whole flight instead of only the aim
  ray. Bots use live projectile bounds and gravity over segmented sweeps, check
  moving teammates and their future splash exposure with world cover, and can
  recognize incoming explosive danger from a direct path, predicted world
  impact or slow/resting fuse. Lateral visibility samples also let them acquire
  a genuinely exposed shoulder without seeing through cover.
- Improved moment-to-moment decisions with weapon-aware engagement distance,
  bounded pursuit from a fading cached position and velocity, extra priority
  for enemies threatening a friendly objective carrier, and an opposite-side
  dodge when the first escape direction is unsafe. Escorts use nav-projected
  trailing formation lanes, and defenders hold a capture perimeter rather than
  standing on the objective.
- Added deterministic distance-aware roles for Standard CTF, One Flag, Arena
  CTF and Arena One Flag, including returners, carrier interceptors, escorts,
  attackers, defenders and ordered per-instance assault-point chains. Freeze
  Tag uniquely pairs rescuers with bodies and raises urgency as a team nears
  elimination; DeadZone uniquely distributes artifacts and adapts escort,
  intercept and zone-contest strength to ownership and deadlock state.
- Added persistent match wins, boss unlocks, next-tier unlocks, final campaign
  completion, replay support, and a separate Arena-only progress reset.
- Added a confirmation step for progress reset, recommended-action focus for
  keyboard and controller navigation, distinct retry and replay choices,
  explicit draw results, and clearer tier/progression context.
- Made Arena match startup and results dependable: switching between Mission
  and Arena is safe while the game mode reloads, authored bots join at their
  exact intended skill, Duel contenders enter before the countdown, final
  scores come from the authoritative match state, and the campaign result
  screen can no longer be displaced by the ordinary multiplayer summary.
- Added an Arena-only presentation flow: delayed challenge-card launch,
  automatic cinematic entrance without a ready prompt, frozen end-of-match
  tableau, collision-aware victor orbit with focused depth of field, staged
  post-match ledger, and animated match, progress, boss, and tier updates on
  return to the ladder.
- Arena's private-server rules are now transactional. Completing, retrying,
  cancelling, disconnecting, encountering a recoverable error, or quitting
  restores the player's previous multiplayer map, limits, and bot settings.
- Moved the final Makron duel to the previously unused official
  Stroggenomenon tournament arena, avoiding a repeated boss map and giving the
  campaign a distinct final battleground.
- Added a stock-only campaign manifest so maps, modes, limits, skills, and bot
  lineups can be validated without duplicating retail map content.

## Known Limitations

- The campaign currently focuses on combat-driven modes. Objective-aware bots
  are available in ordinary multiplayer for Standard CTF, One Flag, Arena CTF,
  Arena One Flag, Freeze Tag rescue and DeadZone, but campaign cards,
  progression, and result flow have not yet been authored for those modes.
- Attack & Defend, Overload, Harvester and Domination do not yet expose the
  complete authoritative runtime state and scoring rules needed for dependable
  objective choices. Bots intentionally fail closed to general combat, pickup
  and roaming behavior in those modes rather than pretending to play an
  objective they cannot score reliably.
